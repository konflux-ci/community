# Privacy Statement for the Konflux Project

## Scope of This Notice

This Privacy Statement is intended to describe the Konflux Project's privacy practices and provide information about the choices you have regarding the ways in which information collected by the Konflux Project is used and disclosed. For convenience, the Konflux Project is referred to in this document as "Konflux".

## Our Commitment to Privacy

At Konflux, your privacy is important to us. To better protect your privacy, we have provided this Statement explaining our information practices and the choices you can make about the way your personal information is collected, used and disclosed. To make this Statement easy to find, we have made it available on our homepage and at every location where personally-identifiable information may be requested.

## The Information We Collect

This Privacy Statement applies to all information collected by or submitted to Konflux, including personal data. "Personal data" is data that can be used to identify an individual.  
Konflux collects personal data when:

* you browse any of our community websites.
* you submit questions or comments to us.

Konflux may also collect personal data from individuals (with their consent) at conventions, trade shows and expositions.  
The types of personal data collected may include (but are not limited to):

* **Identifiers:** Unique Online Identifiers (e.g., User ID, Client ID, Cookie ID).
* **Internet or Other Electronic Network Activity Information:** Browsing history, pages viewed, time spent on pages, click patterns, referring websites (Traffic Sources/Referrers), and search terms.
* **Device and Usage Data:** Browser type, operating system (Platform), device type, screen size, IP address (or anonymized IP address), and language settings.

## Using (Processing) Your Personal Data

Konflux uses the personal data you provide to:

* To analyze website traffic and usage patterns (Website Analytics).
* To improve and optimize the performance and content of our website.
* To understand user journeys and how users interact with our features.

## Sharing Your Personal Data

Unless you consent, Konflux will never process or share the personal data you provide to us except as described below.  
Konflux may disclose your personal data to third parties under any of the following circumstances:

* As required by law (such as responding to a valid subpoena, warrant, audit, or agency action, or to prevent fraud).  
* For research activities, including the production of statistical reports (such aggregated information is used to describe our services and is not used to contact the subjects of the report).

## Cookies and Other Browser Information

Konflux's online services automatically capture IP addresses. We use IP addresses to help diagnose problems with our servers, to administer our website, and to help ensure the security of your interaction with our services. Your IP address is used to help identify you and your location, in order to provide you data and content from our services as quickly as possible. It is in Konflux’s legitimate business interest to maximize the efficiency and effectiveness of its services for all users.  
As part of offering and providing customizable and personalized services, Konflux uses cookies to store and sometimes track information about you. A cookie is a small amount of data that is sent to your browser from a Web server and stored on your computer's hard drive.
Generally, we use cookies to:

1. Estimate audience size. Each browser accessing Konflux is given a unique cookie that is used to determine the extent of repeat usage and usage by a registered user versus by an unregistered user.  
2. Measure certain traffic patterns, which areas of Konflux's network of websites you have visited, and your visiting patterns in the aggregate. We use this research to understand how our user's habits are similar or different from one another so that we can make each new experience on Konflux website a better one. We may use this information to better personalize the content you and other users will see on our sites.

If you do not want your personal information to be stored by cookies, you can configure your browser so that it always rejects these cookies or asks you each time if you accept them or not. However, you must understand that the use of cookies may be necessary to provide certain services, and choosing to reject cookies will reduce the performance and functionality of the site. Your browser documentation includes instructions explaining how to enable, disable or delete cookies at the browser level (usually located in the “Help”, “Tools” or “Edit” facility).

## Our Commitment to Data Security

Konflux trains its administrators on our privacy policy guidelines and makes our privacy policy available to our partners. Our website uses Transport Layer Security (TLS) technology, which encrypts your personal data when you send your personal information on our website. In addition, Konflux and its partners enter into confidentiality agreements which require that care and precautions be taken to prevent loss, misuse, or disclosure of your personal data.

## Public Forums Reminder

Konflux makes chat rooms and/or mailing lists available to its users. Please remember that any information that is disclosed in these areas becomes public information. Exercise caution when deciding to disclose your personal data. Although we value individual ideas and encourage free expression, Konflux reserves the right to take necessary action to preserve the integrity of these areas, such as removing any posting that is in breech of our [Code of Conduct][coc]. It is in Konflux’s legitimate business interests to provide all users an accurate record of data and content provided in the public forums it maintains and uses; to maintain the integrity of that data and content for historical, scientific, and research purposes; and to provide an environment for the free exchange of ideas relevant and constructive to the development and propagation of open source software.

## Our Commitment to Children's Online Privacy

Out of special concern for children's privacy, Konflux does not knowingly accept online personal information from children under the age of 13\. Konflux does not knowingly allow children under the age of 13 to become registered members of our sites. Konflux does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information about children under 13\.  
In the event that Konflux ever decides to expand its intended site audience to include children under the age of 13, those specific web pages will, in accordance with the requirements of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), be clearly identified and provide an explicit privacy notice addressed to children under 13\. In addition, Konflux will provide an appropriate mechanism to obtain parental approval, allow parents to subsequently make changes to or request removal of their children's personal information, and provide access to any other information as required by law.

## About Links to Other Sites

This site contains links to other sites. Konflux does not control the information collection of sites that can be reached through links from Konflux Website. If you have questions about the data collection procedures of linked sites, please contact these sites directly.

## Your Rights and Choices in the EEA

Where the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 (“GDPR”) applies to the processing of your personal data, especially when you access the website from a country in the European Economic Area (“EEA”), you have the following rights, subject to some limitations, against Konflux:  
The right to access your personal data;
The right to rectify the personal data we hold about you;  
The right to erase your personal data;  
The right to restrict our use of your personal data;  
The right to object to our use of your personal data;  
The right to receive your personal data in a usable electronic format and transmit it to a third party (also known as the right of data portability); and  
The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you would like to exercise any of these rights, you may do so via our Personal Data Request Form. Please understand, however, the rights enumerated above are not absolute in all cases. Where the GDPR applies, you also have the right to withdraw any consent you have given to uses of your personal data. If you wish to withdraw consent that you have previously provided to Konflux, you may do so via our Personal Data Request Form. However, the withdrawal of consent will not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal
